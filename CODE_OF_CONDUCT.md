# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

StatePort aims for a respectful, evidence-led collaboration environment. We
expect participants to be professional, constructive, and considerate of
different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, discrimination, personal
attacks, deliberate disclosure of private or confidential information,
unwanted contact, and attempts to bypass the project's safety or privacy
boundaries.

## Scope

This Code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, review, events, and direct project-related communication.

## Enforcement

The project owner is the initial enforcement authority under
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Before public contribution intake opens, the
project will publish a private reporting route. Until then, no public
community space is offered and this policy must not be read as an invitation to
send sensitive reports through public channels.

Maintainers may request correction, remove content, restrict participation, or
take other proportionate action. Decisions should protect affected people and
avoid unnecessary disclosure of personal information.

## Good-faith reporting

Reports made in good faith will be handled as privately as the available
reporting route permits. Do not include security vulnerabilities in conduct
reports; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
