# Contributor licence agreement policy

## Status

StatePort requires a written contributor licence agreement (CLA) before it
accepts external contributions. **No signing or verification route is
published yet, so external contributions are not currently accepted.**

This policy is a statement of the project's intended contribution process. It
is not itself a signed agreement, legal advice, or a grant of contributor
rights.

## Why a CLA

The public code is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The project may make a
commercial exception available only through a separate written agreement from
the copyright holder. A CLA is required so that accepted contributors grant
the project enough rights to distribute their contribution under the public
licence and, where agreed, under a separately negotiated commercial licence.

## Minimum agreement terms

The published individual and corporate agreement forms must require that a
contributor:

- has authority to submit the contribution;
- grants the copyright and patent permissions needed to distribute the
  contribution under the repository's licences and an agreed commercial
  exception, if one is later offered;
- identifies third-party material and its licence;
- does not knowingly submit secrets, private data, restricted material, or
  material that infringes another party's rights; and
- accepts that the project owner may decide whether to merge, modify,
  redistribute, or decline the contribution.

The actual agreement will state its governing terms, signatory requirements,
and any corporate authorization requirements. It must be reviewed and
approved before intake begins.

## Data handling

Do not commit signed agreements, signatures, postal addresses, identity
documents, or other personal data to this repository. The future verification
route must retain only the minimum information needed to demonstrate a valid
agreement and must publish its retention and access policy.

## Commercial exceptions

No commercial exception or dual licence is granted by this policy. It exists
only if the copyright holder and the requesting party execute a separate
written agreement.
