# Support policy

## Current availability

Signed alpha artifacts are public. There is no service-level agreement or paid
support offering, and complete-product/native qualification remains open.

## Bug reports

Public, redacted product bugs can be reported in
[StatePort Source issues](https://github.com/lennertvhoy/StatePort-Source/issues).
The public repository and enabled issue intake were checked through GitHub on
2026-09-05; no response-time commitment is implied. Include the version, target,
a minimal synthetic reproduction, and relevant diagnostic codes. Consult the
[troubleshooting guide](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) first.

Do not include credentials, learner material, real instances or security exploit
details in public issues. Private vulnerability reporting is still disabled;
see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for this unresolved intake boundary.

## Boundaries

- Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public
  support channels.
- The local single-user source-evaluation shape and its gaps
  are recorded in [docs/LOCAL_ALPHA_LIMITATIONS.md](docs/LOCAL_ALPHA_LIMITATIONS.md).
- Community conduct follows [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Commercial exceptions or managed-service terms require a separate written
  agreement; this repository does not offer one.
- Documentation and source code may be inspected under their stated licences,
  but that does not create an obligation to provide assistance.
