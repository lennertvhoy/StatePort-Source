# Support policy

## Current availability

StatePort has no public support channel, service-level agreement, paid support
offering, or public release yet.

## Bug reports

No public bug-reporting route is active. Issue templates in the source tree
prepare a possible future intake path but do not activate it. For now, consult
the [troubleshooting guide](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) and keep a minimal,
redacted reproduction for the future published route. Do not place private
data, credentials, learner material, real instances, or security details in a
public issue, patch, log, screenshot, or improvised channel.

Before public release, the project will publish a support route, supported
version matrix, expected scope, and a way to distinguish product support from
security disclosure. Until then, do not send private data or production
incidents through a public issue or repository comment.

## Boundaries

- Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public
  support channels.
- The supported Linux-first, local-single-user evaluation shape and its gaps
  are recorded in [docs/LOCAL_ALPHA_LIMITATIONS.md](docs/LOCAL_ALPHA_LIMITATIONS.md).
- Community conduct follows [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Commercial exceptions or managed-service terms require a separate written
  agreement; this repository does not offer one.
- Documentation and source code may be inspected under their stated licences,
  but that does not create an obligation to provide assistance.
